## Before you start

- Everything here was pushed on 10-16 (up to `b0d791d`). Before using the
  deployed site, check Railway has deployed it: `/auth/gmail` on the API should
  no longer answer. Railway runs the migrations itself on each deploy
  (`migrate:prod` in `railway.json`), including `0101` for step 18.
- On localhost, run `npm run db:migrate`. Riham's merges added migrations
  `0097` to `0100` (without them her reconcile, phone and vendor notes changes
  fail), and `0101` gives the starting chart's accounts their detail types, so
  the Balance Sheet groups properly (steps 17 and 18). Accounts a client added
  or renamed with no detail type still land under Other Current Assets or Other
  Current Liabilities.
- Sign in as a **firm admin** (steps 1 and 33 need it). Locally that is
  `admin@example.com`.
- Have one posted journal entry ready to reverse (step 11).
- For steps 23 to 25, have two or three open bills from **different vendors**,
  and a bank account with a few checks already written on it.
- For step 26, use a company with transactions in **two years** (last year and
  this one), or post a couple dated last year.

## Reports

- [ ] **13. One title (55).** Open Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, Trial Balance and
  General Ledger. Each shows its name once, in the report header, with no
  second heading above it.
- [ ] **14. Sort the General Ledger (64).** Click a column heading (Name,
  Memo / Description, Debit). The lines inside each account sort A to Z; click
  again for Z to A; a third click goes back to date order. An arrow on the
  heading shows the direction.
- [ ] **15. Printing (55, 65, 66).** Print the Balance Sheet. A preview opens

  QuickBooks; switch and the page changes shape. Print or Save as PDF: the line

  each section is a heading row.

## Account types (57)

- [ ] **16. Chart of Accounts.** Accounting → Chart of Accounts. The Account Type
  column reads Bank, Fixed Assets, Credit Card, Long Term Liabilities and so on,
  not "asset" or "liability". Edit an account: choose the account type, then a
  detail type from that type's list.
- [ ] **17. Balance Sheet layout.** Reports → Balance Sheet. Assets are laid out
  as Current Assets (bank accounts, receivables, other current), Fixed Assets
  and Other Assets; liabilities as Current and Long-Term. Each block has a
  subtotal, and the totals are the same as before.

- [ ] **18. New companies start grouped (57, added 10-16).** Add a client (or
  open one made before) and run its Balance Sheet: Cash on Hand and Operating
  Bank Account are under Bank Accounts, Equipment and Accumulated Depreciation

  company's starting accounts had no detail type and all landed under Other

  fill-in on their starting accounts; accounts a client added or renamed are
  left as they were. One knock-on: Depreciation Expense moves to Other Expense
  on the P&L, as in QuickBooks (net income is the same).

# Part 2: Follow-ups from the 10-10 meeting

What was asked for in the 10-10 meeting and built since.

## Menus and records

- [ ] **20. Payroll is gone (35).** The sidebar, + New and the search box have no
  payroll. "Add contractor" is under Vendors in + New, and Contractors and the
  1099 report are still under Accounts Payable. The payroll pages still open
  by address, and nothing was deleted, in case a payroll partner comes later.
- [ ] **21. Audit history waits for a save (42).** + New → Check, before saving:
  More → Audit history is grayed out, and hovering it reads "Save it first;
  history starts when it is saved". Save, and it opens. Same on Journal Entry,
  Bank Deposit and Expense. In the 10-10 demo it did nothing on a new record.
- [ ] **22. Equipment in the bank picker (39).** The account types from step 16
  now decide what counts as a bank account, so Equipment can only reach a bank
  picker if it was set up under Banking in that company. Open Banking in the
  company from the 10-10 demo: if Equipment is listed there, that is the cause.

## Paying bills by check (36)

- [ ] **23. Numbered checks.** Accounts Payable → Pay Bills. Tick bills from two
  or three vendors, set the method to Check. "Starting check no." is filled in
  with the bank's next number (one after the highest check written on it, from
  Check, Expense or an earlier bill payment). Pay. A panel lists one check per
  vendor, numbered in a row from the starting number, vendors in name order.
- [ ] **24. Print them.** In that panel, "Print on check stock" opens a PDF with
  one check per page, laid out as a QuickBooks voucher check: check on top, two
  stubs below listing the bills paid. "Print on plain paper" is the same checks
  with labels and lines, for a test print. "Done" closes the panel.
- [ ] **25. Print again later.** Pay Bills → Past payments, open one of the
  payments just made. It shows "Check no." and the same two print buttons. A
  check from + New → Check also has "Print on check stock".
  *Not checked yet:* the positions are the standard voucher layout, not measured
  against the firm's own check stock. Print one on plain paper, hold it over a
  blank check, and see that the date, payee, amount and signature line fall in
  the right places before printing real checks.

## Year end (37)

- [ ] **26. Trial Balance and General Ledger start a new year.** Reports → Trial
  Balance, dated this year. Income and expense accounts show this fiscal year
  only; last year's profit is in Retained Earnings, and a note under the table
  says so with the amount. Debits still equal credits, and Retained Earnings
  matches the Balance Sheet. Click an income account: its ledger starts at the
  first day of the fiscal year. Nothing is posted to make this happen, the same
  as QuickBooks.

## "This year" is the client's fiscal year (40)

- [ ] **27.** Setup → Entity, set "Fiscal year starts" to July and save. Then:
  Accounting → Transactions with "This year" runs July 1 to June 30. So do the
  "This year" filters on Bills, Bill Payments, Payments, Credit Memos, Vendor
  Credits, Banking transactions and Reconcile history. Profit & Loss, Statement
  of Cash Flows, Cash Activity and Customer Statement open on July 1. The
  General Ledger keeps both: "This Year" is still the calendar year, and "This
  Fiscal Year" is July to June. Set it back to January afterwards. Riham built
  the same thing at the same time; the two were merged.

## Vendor balances and long lists (41)

- [ ] **28. Open balance is what is owed.** Pay part of a bill. Vendors list:
  that vendor's Open balance drops by the amount paid. Add a vendor credit or
  pay more than a bill: that comes off too. The vendor page and its side list
  show the same figure. Before, it was the sum of the bill totals, paid or not.
  The figure itself is Riham's server-side version, which replaced Aheed's on
  merging.
- [ ] **29. Lists past 50.** In a company with more than 50 bills or invoices,
  the Bills and Invoices lists, and the vendor and customer pages, show all of
  them. Before, they stopped at the newest 50 without saying so.

## Behind the scenes (nothing to click)

- [ ] **30. Backups (43).** `npm run db:backup` copies the database to
  `backups/`; `npm run db:backup:verify` restores that copy into a throwaway
  database and counts every table's rows. Tried against a test database only.
  Railway has no backup schedule set; running it once against production is in
  [`../guides/backups.md`](../guides/backups.md).
- [ ] **31. Staff and imported bank lines.** A staff login is refused if it tries
  to edit an imported bank transaction, in the service as well as the route.
  Nothing showed this before, because the route already refused staff.
- [ ] **32. Faizan's first video written up (34).** Every change he asked for is
  an item in [`../meetings/2026-10-12-faizan-video-1.md`](../meetings/2026-10-12-faizan-video-1.md),
  and Part 1 is the result. His PDF and check stubs are still to go through.
- [ ] **33. Only a firm admin connects Gmail (28, added 10-16).** AI → Document
  Inbox has a Connect Gmail button for a firm admin; nobody else sees it. It
  opens Google's consent page in a new tab. Before, anyone who could reach the
  API could open `/auth/gmail` and put their own mailbox in the firm's place,
  without signing in. That address is gone, and Google's reply is taken only if
  it carries a 10-minute hand-off from a firm admin. Each connection is in
  Setup → Activity Log → Sign-ins and users. A Gmail already connected stays connected.
  Neither localhost nor the deployed API has Google's credentials file yet, so
  today the button answers "Gmail is not set up on this server" in red instead
  of opening Google. That still shows the button and who sees it; going all the
  way through needs the file at `secrets/gmail-credentials.json` (or
  `GMAIL_CREDENTIALS_PATH`).
