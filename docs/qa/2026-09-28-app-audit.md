### 1. AI Inbox fills in account guesses the engine rejected
- **Where:** AI → Document Inbox → review a statement or invoice.
- **What happens:** A row badged **Unclassified** still has an account pre-selected. On the March statement, `CHASE CARD PAYMENT` shows *Unclassified* but the dropdown is already set to *Business Credit Card Clearing*.
- **Why:** An older fallback fuzzy-matches the model's raw text against account names by substring whenever the engine returns nothing.
  `apps/web/src/pages/banking/EmailImportReviewPage.tsx:265` (bank rows) and `:383` (invoice lines).
- **Why it matters:** It defeats the rule in CLAUDE.md that a rejected AI guess is never filled in. Substring matching is also unsafe: a model answer of "Sales" matches **Sales Tax Payable** (a liability) before Sales Revenue.
- **Fix:** Delete both fallback branches. If the engine returns nothing, leave the row blank.

### 2. New invoices are due the day they're issued, whatever the terms
- **Where:** Accounts Receivable → New invoice.
- **What happens:** Terms default to *Net 30* but Due date equals the invoice date. Changing terms to *Net 60* leaves the due date unchanged. Every new invoice is overdue from day one.
- **Why:** `due_date` is initialised to today and nothing recalculates it. `apps/web/src/pages/invoices/InvoiceNewPage.tsx:28`. The customer's `default_terms_days` is also ignored.
- **Fix:** Copy the Bill form, which gets this right (choosing Net 30 sets due date +30). Default the terms from the selected customer.

### 3. The 1099 report leaves out direct payments to contractors
- **Where:** Reports → 1099.
- **What happens:** Only bill payments are counted. Expenses paid straight to a 1099 vendor are missing — **$1,221** for 2026:

  | Vendor | Bill payments (counted) | Direct expenses (missing) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Dana Brooks Design | 1,446.00 | 317.00 |
  | Demo Contractor B | 391.00 | 540.00 |
  | Summit Legal | 1,657.00 | 364.00 |

- **Why it matters:** Under-reporting on a 1099-NEC is a compliance problem.
- **Fix:** Include `expense_transactions` for 1099 vendors, excluding card payments (those go on a 1099-K). Flag vendors that cross $600 and vendors with no tax ID.

### 4. Management Reports shows $0 revenue in every month
- **Where:** Reports → Management.
- **What happens:** "Revenue by month" is $0.00 for all 12 months, while the header says $85,115.
- **Why:** The revenue-account filter sits in a `LEFT JOIN` condition, so every line of every journal entry is summed — and balanced entries net to zero.
  `apps/api/src/services/reports/managementReportService.ts:51`
- **Fix:** Move `coa.account_type = 'revenue'` into the `WHERE`/`FILTER`, or sum only when `coa.id IS NOT NULL`.

### 5. Profit & Loss: "Net Operating Income" is wrong
- **Where:** Reports → Profit & Loss.
- **What happens:** *Net Operating Income* shows **$85,115.00** — the same as total income. It should be **$8,868.46**. *Net Income* below it is correct, so the report contradicts itself.
- **Fix:** Net operating income = gross profit − operating expenses.

### 6. General Ledger running balance is out of order
- **Where:** Reports → General Ledger.
- **What happens:** Within a day, the balance column doesn't follow the rows. On 1/2/26 the first row is a $5,000 credit yet shows a balance of $500; on 1/10/26 a $9,000 credit makes the balance go *up*.
- **Why:** The server computes the running balance ordered by date then creation time; the page re-sorts same-day rows by `line_id`, a random UUID, keeping the old balances. `apps/web/src/pages/reports/GeneralLedgerPage.tsx:287`
- **Fix:** Keep the server's order (use its tiebreaker, or a stable sort by date only).

### 7. Inventory never reaches the books
- **What happens:** Inventory values stock at **$16,058.65**; the Inventory account in the ledger is **$0.00**. Cost of Goods Sold on the P&L is **$0** despite sales orders. Item receipts have an empty *Bill #*, so receiving stock creates no payable.
- **Why it matters:** The balance sheet understates assets and the P&L overstates gross profit.
- **Fix (larger piece of work):** Post journal entries on receipt (Dr Inventory / Cr AP or GRNI) and on sale (Dr COGS / Cr Inventory), and let invoice lines reference inventory items rather than free text.

### 8. Bank "Categorize" pre-fills an arbitrary account
- **Where:** Accounting → Bank Transactions → Categorize.
- **What happens:** The account box pre-fills **5010 Cost of Goods Sold** for "Transfer – Payroll" — simply the first account in the list. One quick *Submit* misclassifies the transaction silently.
- **Fix:** Start blank, or use the auto-coding engine's suggestion (see P1 #2).

## Demo-data problems (not code)

These make the demo look broken but aren't bugs:

- **Every bank account holds the same 6 transactions** (`db/seeds/0013_demo_banking.sql` copies them per account), so all five show an identical bank balance.
- **Operating Bank Account is −$41,214.57**, driven by ~$86.5k of seeded adjustment entries.
- **The Business Credit Card is an asset account** (1024 "Business Credit Card Clearing"). A card is a liability; this is also why the auto-coder declines to code card payments for this client.
- **1099 vendors have no tax IDs,** and a contractor is used as a stock supplier on purchase orders.

## Verified working

- **All 92 routes** render with no JavaScript errors, no failed API calls, and no blank pages.
- **All 169 API endpoints** respond successfully for both clients, none slower than 1.5s.
- **Books integrity (14 checks):** every entry balances; trial balance balances for both clients; AR and AP subledgers tie to their control accounts; no entry uses another client's account; source documents match their journal entries; nothing is over-applied; every approved import has journal entries; every coding rule points to a live account.
- **Tenant isolation:** requesting one client's records through another client's URL returns nothing for all 26 detail routes.
- **Forms:** Bill terms set the due date; Receive Payment lists the customer's open invoices; invoice numbers auto-fill; the journal assigns the next number.
- **Mobile:** none of 8 key pages overflow at phone width; the sidebar becomes a drawer; wide tables scroll inside their container.

## Suggestions

- **Unify the rule systems:** make bank rules a layer of the auto-coding engine so one place manages "how this client codes X".
- **Run the engine everywhere transactions are categorized** — bank feed, integrations and AI Inbox should share one suggestion pipeline and one badge.
- **Add a "what's owed" dashboard:** cash by account, AR and AP aging, month-to-date P&L, items needing review.
- **Make list defaults match their headers,** or show "showing last 3 months of N" so hidden records are obvious.
- **Show document numbers everywhere a link appears** (JE #69, SO-4003) instead of ID fragments.
- **Add a print stylesheet** and a real invoice PDF template.
- **Lock or link asset-purchase journal entries** to the fixed-asset register so edits can't desync them.
- **Replace the demo bank seed** with distinct, realistic transactions per account and a liability credit-card account, so the demo reflects how the app actually behaves.
- **Turn the books-integrity checks into a scheduled job** or an admin page, so drift is caught automatically.
